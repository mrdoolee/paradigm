Pixel art illustration drawn in chalk on a dark green school blackboard, for an educational game set at a 1920s geology meeting.
A simple map seen from above: the east coast of South America on the left edge and the west coast of Africa on the right edge, both at their present-day positions, far apart,
with the wide Atlantic Ocean between them drawn as rows of small wavy chalk lines.
One long, narrow land bridge crosses the middle of the ocean straight from South America to Africa, drawn as a solid pale chalk strip a little thicker than a road.
Two small Mesosaurus (slender freshwater reptiles with long thin tails) walk along the land bridge.
The land bridge stays in the middle third of the picture; the top and bottom of the ocean are only wavy lines.
No text, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no people, no frame.
Crisp chunky pixels, limited palette: dark green board, white chalk, pale yellow chalk, pale blue chalk. Flat colours, no gradients, no blur.

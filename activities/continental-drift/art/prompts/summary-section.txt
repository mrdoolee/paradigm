Pixel art educational diagram for middle-school students: a simple side cross-section of the Earth's outer layers, in the style of the reference image (the game's ocean-floor cross-section).
From left to right: under the sea, a mid-ocean ridge where two oceanic plates move apart from each other while orange-red magma rises up from the mantle into the gap in the middle;
the right oceanic plate moves to the right and bends down into the mantle beneath the edge of a continent at a deep ocean trench;
on the continent, just inland from the trench, a volcano with a little glow at its top.
Thin plates (dark grey-blue rock) lie on top of a thick warm orange-brown mantle. A few simple white arrows show the plates moving apart at the ridge and the plate sinking at the trench.
Blue sea above the ridge and trench, pale sky band at the very top. No text, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no people, no frame.
Crisp chunky pixels, limited flat colours, no gradients, no anti-aliasing.
